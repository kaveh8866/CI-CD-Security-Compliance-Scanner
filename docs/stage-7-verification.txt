3. COST AND BENCHMARK BASIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tested footprint: 1 vCPU (container capped at 0.25), 1 GiB RAM, 50 GB disk,
negligible egress (~11 KB JSON / ~34 KB SARIF per scan).

Assumptions: Linux on-demand list prices, November 2026, 730 h/month, excluding
tax, backups and support; free-tier allowances applied where stated.

  OCI Always Free   E2.1.Micro 1 OCPU / 1 GB; 50 GB inside the 200 GB block
                    allowance; egress inside 10 TB/month -> $0/month.
  OCI trial credits Stage 5 ran entirely on trial credits -> $0 cash.
  AWS eu-central-1  t3.micro $0.012/h (~$8.76) + 50 GB gp3 @ $0.0952 (~$4.76)
                    + first 100 GB egress free -> ~$13.52/month.
                    1 TB egress adds ~$83.16 -> ~$96.68/month.
  GCP us-central1   e2-micro $0.008376/h (~$6.12) + 50 GB pd-standard @ $0.04
                    (~$2.00) + 100 GB egress @ $0.12 (~$12.00) ->
                    ~$20.12 on-demand; ~$12.68 with free-tier compute.

Benchmarks quoted from measured runs: 181 MB image, 19.19 MiB idle RSS, 24.5 MiB
peak, 54 ms live scan (OCI micro) / 114.6 ms local for 10 files and 21 findings.

Caveat: these are list-price estimates for the tested shape, not a bill. Egress
tiers, free-tier eligibility by account age/region and CPU-credit behaviour on
t3/e2 shared-core instances change the real number. Re-measure after migrating.
